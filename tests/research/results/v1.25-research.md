# Research for v1.25.0 (wolfmux-618): where time and tokens go, memory and context

Base: port branch @ 01cdee4, Claude Code 2.1.325, 2026-11-16. Scripts and raw numbers: `tests/research/` on
`ai/memory-management/research-20261116-111031` (`results/runs.jsonl`, `results/probes.jsonl`).

## The short version

1. **wolfmux's own latency is no longer the problem.** Task created to report read by the lead: 15.6 s and 20.7 s
   (two runs), of which wolfmux is about 3 s. What costs is tokens per request and compaction.
2. **Compaction thrash is the worst failure, and I reproduced it.** The CLI compacts at `window - 33k`. After a
   compaction the conversation is `fixed part + 19-47k kept`. The port worker (Opus, 200k) restarted each time at
   123-147k of a 166k limit and stood still 514 s for 4 compactions in 11 minutes. A Haiku worker with a 100k
   limit compacted 10 times in 5 minutes, then the CLI gave up ("Autocompact is thrashing"), the worker sat idle
   at 3 of 4 commits, and nobody was told. The same task without compaction: 4 commits in 94 s.
3. **The fixed part can drop by a third with three settings, measured.** Worker first request 81,024 -> 54,289;
   lead 115,652 -> 70,017. Same task, same result.
4. **The context watch never fired in any run**: it divided by 1,000,000 while the CLI compacted at 67k.
5. **A fresh conversation does continue a half-done task** (restart after commit 2 of 4: bearings in 9.6 s and
   2 requests, finished correctly), with no checkpoint. On a small task, one run.

Runs used: 4 team runs + 8 probes = 12. Everything is destroyed; no tmux process is left on the socket.

### 1.4 What a compaction keeps and costs

| Conversation | Limit | Compacted at | Kept (postTokens) | Next request | Room left | Standstill |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| port worker, 4 times in 11 min | 200k | 165-171k | 36-47k | 123-147k | 20-43k | 49, 91, 183, 190 s |
| lead, twice | not known | 449k, 278k | 38k | 130-131k | large | 75-91 s |
| run 4 worker, 10 times in 5 min | 100k | 66-89k | 19-37k | 62-85k | 4-5k | 19-31 s each, 231 s |

- The CLI compacts at about **window - 33k** (166k of 200k; 67k of 100k).
- After it: `fixed part + kept`. The kept part is a summary (8-11k tokens) plus a verbatim tail. The rules, the
  listings and the SessionStart hook text are attached again every time (run 4: 10 x about 95k characters).
- Every compaction rewrites the prompt cache: run 4 wrote 563,895 cache tokens, run 3 (same task, no
  compaction) 89,082.
- Run 4 end state: CLI message "Autocompact is thrashing: the context refilled to the limit within 3 turns of
  the previous compact, 3 times in a row", worker idle, task `in_progress`, 1 dirty file, lead not told.
  `teamctl status` showed `0.0k 0%`; the watch file said `{"tokens":65283,"window":1000000,"percent":6}`.
- The PreCompact hook did write the git state into `checkpoint.md` each time. Nothing read it.

### 1.5 Handover (run 3, second task; one run)

`teamctl restart w2` right after its 2nd of 4 commits, in the middle of a turn. New conversation 2.7 s later;
2 requests and 9.6 s to get its bearings (claim + status, then one `cat` of spec, lib, tests, NOTES.md: 19,982
characters); 117k input tokens for that. It did steps 3 and 4 only, got the special case right (status 4), and
closed the task 49 s after the restart. No checkpoint existed (the watch never fired). What it worked from: the
task text shown as `in_progress`, `git log`, and NOTES.md, a step log the task itself demanded.

### 1.6 Other findings

- **Auto memory is shared across worktrees** in 2.1.325: the port worker's first request holds the project's
  `MEMORY.md` (1,169 characters), mine too. Point 7 of your list is out of date on that. A worker can also write
  there.
- **The watch fires inside in-process subagents.** Two of my three research subagents were told to commit and
  checkpoint; one cut its research short "at 80%".
- **A worker cannot run an isolated real-CLI measurement**: the guard refuses `tmux -L <other socket>`, teamctl
  has no verb that types a key, and folder trust is per path. `TEAM_ROLE=lead` from outside is refused too
  ("claims lead but does not run in its pane"); acting as the operator (no TEAM_ROLE) works.
- `WOLFMUX_WORKER_COMPACT_WINDOW` in the environment of `teamctl task --spawn` was ignored (the worker got
  1,000,000); set at `teamctl start` it was applied. I did not trace why.

## Part 3. Proposals for v1.25.0

### Do first

**1. One number, and a compaction that cannot thrash.**
- Problem: 514 s and 231 s of standstill (1.4); the watch at 6% while the CLI compacted; status `0%`.
- Change: per agent `used / limit`, where `limit = compact window - 33k` and `used` is the last request of its
  transcript (or, better, the statusline JSON). Record the fixed part F (first request of the conversation) and
  the kept part K (postTokens of its last compaction, 45k until one happened). Set the window from them instead
  of from the model table: `window >= F + K + 33k + room`, room 150k by default, capped by the model. Warn and
  rotate thresholds are a share of `limit`. Two compactions within 10 requests, or the CLI's thrash message,
  is its own event: tell the lead and hand over (proposal 4). Skip the watch when the hook input belongs to a
  subagent.
- Files: `lib/recall.sh`, `lib/policy.sh` (`worker_compact_window`), `lib/agents.sh` (`agent_context`,
  `write_agent_settings`), `lib/common.sh`, `lib/views.sh`, `lib/dashboard.sh`, ADR after 0033.
- Cost: 2 days. Measure: run 4 again finishes 4 commits with at most 1 compaction; the warning arrives before
  the CLI compacts; status equals `/context` within 5%.
- Risk: the 33k reserve is observed on two windows, not documented; a provider that serves less than it declares.

**2. A smaller fixed part: `trim` learns four things.**
- Problem: worker 81,024, lead 115,652 before any work (1.1); it is re-sent with every request and decides how
  much room a compaction leaves.
- Change, for workers under `trim`: deny the tools a pane agent cannot use (Artifact, AskUserQuestion,
  SendFeedback, ScheduleWakeup, Workflow, ReportFindings, ListAgents); `ENABLE_CLAUDEAI_MCP_SERVERS=false`; a
  policy key for rule globs to leave out, with `teamctl doctor` pointing at a rules folder that repeats another
  in a second language (your `zh/` is a translation of `common/`, so no rule is lost); the same three for the
  lead as an opt-in `lead-context`, minus AskUserQuestion. `full` keeps today's behaviour.
- Files: `lib/policy.sh` (`worker_context_settings`), `lib/agents.sh`, `lib/doctor.sh`, ADR-0034 amendment,
  `tests/permissions.sh`, one real-CLI check.
- Cost: 1 day. Gain, measured: worker first request -33%, summed input -42% on the same task; lead -39%.
- Risk: an owner who wants a connector in a worker (the policy key); a rule that tells a worker to use a denied
  tool. Open: the 123 account-synced plugin skills (about 5k tokens) have no switch I could find.

**3. Tool output stays out of the window.**
- Problem: Bash output is 33% (port) and 40% (lead) of everything added; 10% of the steps make 36% of the growth;
  the CLI clears nothing.
- Change: the PostToolUse hook answers with `updatedToolOutput` when a Bash result is over a limit (4,000
  characters to start): the full text goes to `run/<role>/out/<n>.log`, the model gets the first and last 40
  lines, every line matching `FAIL|rror|panic`, the line count and the path. Lower `bashOutputMaxChars` as a
  backstop. For Codex, which cannot rewrite a result: `teamctl run <cmd>`, a wrapper that does the same.
- Files: `lib/hooks.sh` (answer shape), new `lib/offload.sh`, `bin/teamctl` hot path (it must see the size
  without parsing), protocol text, `tests/`.
- Cost: 2 days, the first half-day to prove `updatedToolOutput` on Bash against the real CLI.
- Measure: characters of Bash results per request on the port worker's kind of task; target under 1,500 mean.
- Risk: a failure hidden in the cut middle; a model that then reads the whole log (it is told the path, so the
  protocol must say: grep it, do not cat it).

**4. Rotation instead of repeated compaction, with a tested hand-over.**
- Problem: compaction keeps 19-47k and costs 20-190 s each time; "restart the worker" has never been tested and
  the agent never wrote a checkpoint in any run.
- Change: `teamctl rotate <role>`: wolfmux writes the hand-over itself, no question to the agent: task text and
  notes, commits since the base, `git status` and diff stat, the agent's last five answers taken from its
  transcript, the path of that transcript and of the output logs. Then a fresh conversation whose SessionStart
  carries it. Triggered by the lead, by the thrash event of proposal 1, and by the agent at its rotate
  threshold when its tree is clean. Protocol: a multi-step task keeps a step log in the repository (run 3's
  fresh worker found its place through one). `restart --continue`: type the wake line when a task is
  `in_progress`.
- Files: `lib/recall.sh`, `lib/agents.sh` (`cmd_restart`), `lib/tasks.sh`, protocol, and
  `tests/research/` grown into a real-CLI hand-over check.
- Cost: 3 days. Baseline to beat: 9.6 s, 2 requests, 117k input tokens to regain bearings. Measure on a task
  with uncommitted work and no step log.
- Risk: a restart in the middle of a write; one green run on a toy task is thin evidence.

### Later

**5. A lead that stays small.** `teamctl done` stores the report as a file; the lead's mail is under 600
characters (summary, tests line, branch, diff stat, path) and says plainly that it is already read. No notice to
the lead for a task whose report it has; no reply needed for "accepted". Protocol: a diff is reviewed by a
subagent that returns a verdict, not `cat` into the lead. A ledger (tasks table + decisions) that SessionStart
re-attaches after a compaction. Numbers: 5 of 12 requests were mail housekeeping (1.3); the lead's Bash output
is 40% and its own tool inputs 20.7% of what it added. Files: `lib/mail.sh`, `lib/tasks.sh`, `lib/inbox.sh`,
protocol. 3 days.

**6. One memory model** (mostly decisions, 1 day):

| Place | Holds | Loaded | Written by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Store notes (`teamctl remember`) | project facts for every agent and CLI | SessionStart: an index, one line per note; body on `teamctl memories <key>` | any agent |
| Auto memory (`MEMORY.md`, per repository) | the owner's own notes | lead: as today. Workers: off (`CLAUDE_CODE_DISABLE_AUTO_MEMORY=1`), so they stop writing into it | owner, lead |
| Lead's private memory (v1.22) | the lead's working notes | already the lead's `autoMemoryDirectory`: the same mechanism as the row above, not a fifth place | that lead |
| claude-mem plugin | the owner's sessions | never in a worker (trim does this); in the lead only if `lead-context` leaves it | plugin |
